# Sales Qualification Add-On — USE ONLY IF GENUINE SIGNAL SURFACED

Trigger to open this: the CHRO unprompted mentioned pain in one of PRANA's core areas
during Section B/C/E of the neutral discovery questionnaire — document access/management,
offboarding document requests, DPDP/compliance readiness, or employee grievance handling.

Never lead with this. Never let it contaminate the neutral research instrument. If no
signal surfaced, skip this file entirely for this meeting — that's a valid, useful outcome
(tells you this account isn't near-term, without wasting their time on a pitch).

---

## Qualification Framework (MEDDIC-lite)

| Dimension | What to establish | Notes from this meeting |
|---|---|---|
| **M**etrics | What quantifiable pain did they name? (time-to-resolve doc requests, attrition tied to offboarding friction, compliance fines/risk exposure) | |
| **E**conomic buyer | Who actually signs off — this CHRO, or CFO/CISO/CEO? | |
| **D**ecision criteria | What would make them choose a vendor? (security cert, DPDP compliance proof, integration with existing HRMS, cost, implementation speed) | |
| **D**ecision process | RFP required? Procurement/legal review steps? Typical vendor eval timeline? | |
| **I**dentify pain | Is the pain acknowledged and prioritized, or just a passing complaint? | |
| **C**hampion | Would this CHRO actively advocate internally, or just "interested"? | |

## PRANA-Specific Signal Checklist
Mark any that came up unprompted (do not ask directly — these should emerge from their
own words in Section B/C/E):

- ☐ Pain around employees losing access to salary slips/Form 16/offer letters after exit
- ☐ Manual/email-based document requests creating HR-ops overhead
- ☐ DPDP Act 2023 compliance gap (erasure, export, consent, grievance workflows)
- ☐ Concern about raw salary data exposure/security in current document storage
- ☐ Interest in employee self-service for career documents
- ☐ Current HRMS (Darwinbox/Keka/Zoho/SAP) lacking a dedicated employee document vault
- ☐ Multi-employer/gig-economy workforce needing portable career documents across employers

## Qualification Verdict
- ☐ **Hot** — clear pain, budget authority present or reachable, timeline within 2 quarters
- ☐ **Warm** — real pain, but budget/timeline/authority unclear — needs nurturing
- ☐ **Cold** — no material pain in PRANA's space, or firmly out of budget cycle
- ☐ **Not qualified** — this account isn't a fit (explain why, so future you doesn't re-chase it)
