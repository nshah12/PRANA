# CHRO In-Meeting Discovery Questionnaire — Neutral Research Instrument

Run this SAME instrument in every meeting, in this order, regardless of who you're meeting.
Consistency is the whole point — it's what makes 30 conversations comparable instead of
30 disconnected anecdotes. Capture answers in the structured format shown (scale/choice/short
text), not free prose, so they can be transferred straight into the tracker.

This is the neutral layer. Do NOT introduce PRANA or any product pitch here — that's
`02_sales_qualification_addon.md`, and only if a genuine signal surfaces on its own.

---

## Section A — Firmographic Context (2 min, fast factual)
Confirms/corrects what you researched in the pre-meeting brief.

- A1. Total headcount: ______
- A2. Headcount trend (last 12 mo): ☐ Growing ☐ Flat ☐ Shrinking
- A3. Geographic footprint: ☐ Single city ☐ Multi-city (same country) ☐ Multi-country
- A4. HR team size: ______
- A5. HR org model: ☐ Centralized ☐ Business-partner (decentralized) ☐ Hybrid

## Section B — "What They Are Doing" (Current State)
- B1. What are your top 3 active HR initiatives right now?
  1. ______  2. ______  3. ______
- B2. What's your current HRMS/HRIS? ______
- B3. What's your current ATS? ______
- B4. How do employees currently access their own documents (salary slips, Form 16, offer letters, experience letters)? ______
- B5. How do you handle document requests after an employee exits? ______
- B6. Do you have a formal DPDP/data-privacy compliance program in place? ☐ Yes, mature ☐ Yes, in progress ☐ Planned ☐ Not yet
- B7. What's your current approach to employee grievance/data-correction requests? ______

## Section C — "How They Are Doing" (Performance / Effectiveness)
Use a 1-5 scale throughout (1 = very dissatisfied, 5 = very satisfied) for comparability.

- C1. Satisfaction with current HRMS/HRIS: 1 2 3 4 5
- C2. Satisfaction with document/records management specifically: 1 2 3 4 5
- C3. Confidence in current DPDP/compliance readiness: 1 2 3 4 5
- C4. Current attrition rate (if shared): ______%
- C5. Average time to resolve an employee document request: ______ (days/hours)
- C6. What metric are YOU personally measured on by leadership? ______
- C7. What's the single biggest recurring HR-ops headache right now? (verbatim quote if possible) ______

## Section D — Forward-Looking Priorities
- D1. Top 3 priorities for the next 12 months:
  1. ______  2. ______  3. ______
- D2. Any planned HR-tech investment/RFP in the next 12 months? ☐ Yes ☐ No ☐ Unsure
  - If yes, in what area? ______
- D3. When does your budget cycle reset? ______
- D4. Who else is involved in HR-tech purchase decisions? (CFO / CISO / IT / CEO) ______

## Section E — Open Capture
- E1. Any unprompted pain point mentioned that doesn't fit above categories: ______
- E2. Notable verbatim quotes worth preserving exactly: ______
- E3. Referrals/introductions offered: ______
